Privacy Policy for What 2 Cook

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Boikoff Developments ("we", "our", or "us") provides the What 2 Cook mobile application (the "App"). This page explains our privacy practices regarding the information we handle when you use our App and your rights concerning that data.

By downloading, installing, or using What 2 Cook, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information Collection and Storage

We believe in privacy by design. What 2 Cook does not require account creation, registration, or login, and does not upload your personal recipe data to external servers.

1.1 Local Storage on Your Device

All recipes, dish names, descriptions, ingredient notes, and preferences you enter are stored strictly locally on your device (using local database or storage mechanisms). We do not have access to your saved recipes, and they are never transmitted to our servers or third parties.

1.2 Device Permissions

What 2 Cook requires minimal device permissions. It does not request access to your device's camera, microphone, contacts, location, or personal photo library.

2. Third-Party Services and Links

2.1 External Search Links (Google & YouTube)

The App offers convenient shortcuts to search for recipe ideas or cooking videos via Google Search or YouTube. When you tap these buttons, the App launches your default external web browser or YouTube app with the dish query. Any interaction with external websites or services is governed by the respective privacy policies of those providers.

2.2 Google Play Services & Advertising

The App may integrate third-party developer libraries to ensure reliability and support ongoing development:

  • Google Play Services: Provides fundamental platform features, licensing checks, and system updates on Android devices. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: To keep the app accessible for free, we may display advertisements powered by Google AdMob. AdMob may automatically collect and use device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), IP addresses, and cookie/app usage data to deliver non-personalized or personalized ads. Google Privacy & Terms and AdMob Data Usage Details

3. Managing Your Ad Preferences

You can control and reset how your Advertising ID is used for ad personalization directly within your Android device settings:

  • Go to Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Choose "Reset advertising ID" or "Delete advertising ID".

4. Children's Privacy

Children's Information:

What 2 Cook is not directed towards children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

5. GDPR and Data Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and related data protection frameworks, you maintain rights regarding your data:

  • Access and Portability: All recipe data is stored locally on your device and is accessible to you at any time directly through the app interface.
  • Deletion (Right to Erasure): You can delete individual recipes directly within the App, or erase all data at once by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or uninstalling the App.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this policy to reflect enhancements or regulatory changes. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
