Privacy Policy for Active All Day

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Boikoff Developments ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Active All Day mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect or store any of your personal identification information, workout records, alarm routines, or custom settings on external servers. All operations are designed to respect your privacy and minimize data exposure.

1.1 Local Data Storage (Device Only)

All settings, alarms, challenges, and progress data are stored locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to us or any unauthorized third party. You can erase this data at any time by clearing the App's data in your Android system settings or by uninstalling the App.

1.2 Third-Party Services

To support the operation and development of the App, we use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you, measure performance, and serve advertisements.

Below are the third-party providers we integrate with, along with links to their Privacy Policies:

  • Google Play Services: Used to provide core Android APIs, app updates, and license checks. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements within the App. AdMob may collect and use data such as your device's Advertising ID, IP address, and cookie information to show personalized or non-personalized ads. Google Privacy & Terms and How AdMob uses data

2. Advertising and Cookies

AdMob may use technologies like cookies, web beacons, and mobile device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) to deliver relevant ads to you. You can manage your ad preferences and opt-out of interest-based ads by adjusting your device's settings:

  • On your Android device, open "Settings" -> "Privacy" or "Google" -> "Ads".
  • Tap on "Opt out of Ads Personalization" or "Reset advertising ID".

3. Children's Privacy

Important Note regarding Children:

Our App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

4. Your Data Protection Rights Under GDPR

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Since we do not transmit or store your personal data on our servers, exercising these rights is straightforward:

  • Access and Portability: All your active data is stored locally on your device's memory. You can view it directly inside the App.
  • Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You can completely erase your data at any time by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.
  • Restriction and Objection: You can stop all data collection by the App simply by uninstalling it. For third-party ad settings, you can adjust your device advertising identifier options.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date at the top. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
